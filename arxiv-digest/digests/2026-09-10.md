## Core
### The Forgotten History of Wave Function Variance Optimization and its Relevance for Neural-Network VMC
Dario Bressanini  ·  quant-ph  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08453
Why: NQS training-methods paper — revisits variance minimization vs. robust alternatives (MAD, Cauchy, L-4) for optimizing neural-network wavefunctions given the heavy-tailed local-energy distributions seen in NN-VMC.

### Successive Phase Transitions from a Composite Fermion Liquid to a Fractional Quantum Hall State at ν=3/2 Driven by In-Plane Magnetic Field
Xinghao Wang, L. N. Pfeiffer, …, K. W. West, Rui-Rui Du  ·  cond-mat.str-el  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08141
Why: Direct quantum-Hall-family result — reports an in-plane-field-induced even-denominator FQH state at ν=3/2 in ultra-high-mobility GaAs, with successive transitions from a composite-fermion liquid to soft- then hard-gap FQH states.

### Decorated electronic kagome lattice in twisted bilayer germanene
Dennis J. Klaassen, Rian A. M. Ligthart, …, Zeila Zanolli, Harold J. W. Zandvliet  ·  cond-mat.mes-hall  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08750
Why: Moiré flat-band correlated-electron system — STM and DFT show twisted bilayer germanene forms large-angle moiré phases, with the metallic (even-parity) stacking hosting an emergent decorated-kagome flat band.

### The First Magic Angle Beyond the Chiral Limit in Twisted Bilayer Graphene
Leonardo A. Navarro-Labastida, Pierre A. Pantaleon, Francisco Guinea, Gerardo G. Naumis  ·  cond-mat.mes-hall  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08347
Why: Moiré flat-band physics — a squared-Hamiltonian treatment beyond the chiral limit explains why the first magic angle in twisted bilayer graphene survives lattice relaxation while higher-order magic angles are destabilized.

### Tunable topological narrow bands in twisted bilayer-trilayer graphene
Dong Wang, Federico Escudero, Zhen Zhan, Shengjun Yuan  ·  cond-mat.mes-hall  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08104
Why: Moiré flat-band system — tight-binding/continuum modeling of four twisted bilayer-trilayer graphene stackings maps flat-band Chern-number topology and its tuning via twist angle, electric field, and Hartree potential.
