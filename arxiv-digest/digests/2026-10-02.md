### A Different Perspective on Superconductivity in Crystalline Graphene: Exploiting Energetics
Ke Wang, Shicong Song, K. Levin  ·  cond-mat.supr-con  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40104
Why: Energetic "two-parent" framework explaining why moiré/crystalline-graphene superconductivity sits near first-order boundaries between isospin-ordered metals, independent of the pairing mechanism.

### Qantum Well Resonant Tunneling Diode Probe of Correlated States in Twisted Bilayer MoS$_2$

### Atomic-Scale Imaging of Lattice Relaxation and Topological Flat Bands in Helical Trilayer Graphene
