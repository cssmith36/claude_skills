# arXiv digest — 2026-11-25    (4 core, 2 relevant)

## Core
### Quantum-Metric-Driven Hollow Wigner Crystals
Zekun Zhuang  ·  cond-mat.str-el  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29317
Why: Wigner crystallization in a three-band continuum model. Hartree–Fock finds "hollow" Wigner crystals, with density depleted at the lattice sites, driven by the quantum metric even when the Berry curvature is zero. Exchange interactions change a lot across the hollow-to-conventional crossover.

### Symmetry classes of hyperdeterminant wavefunctions: Application to phase transitions between a bosonic fractional Chern insulator and superfluids
Guan-Lin Lin, Xiaodong Hu, Di Xiao, Ying Ran  ·  cond-mat.str-el  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28276
Why: Quantum Hall family plus VMC. The authors optimize symmetric hyperdeterminant variational wavefunctions to study the transition between a bosonic fractional Chern insulator and a superfluid. They report much better accuracy than standard parton states. Not neural, but useful as a variational-ansatz baseline for FQH-type states.

### A Fractional Chern Semimetal: Exact Wavefunction and Conformal Criticality
Fa-Jie Wang, Yang Qi, Zhi-Da Song  ·  cond-mat.str-el  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31234
Why: Quantum Hall family. A Gutzwiller-projected parton tensor-network state at the point where the anyon gap closes. It keeps a many-body Chern number of 1/3, and its modular data matches the ν=1/3 Laughlin state.

### Quantum-geometry-tuned Mott transition and emergent heavy fermions in a topological flat band
Ya-Hui Zhang  ·  cond-mat.str-el  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31242
Why: Correlated topological flat band. DMFT on overcomplete coherent orbitals reproduces the observed cascade and compressibility sawtooth. At ν=−2, a Mott-to-heavy-Fermi-liquid transition is tuned by how much of the Brillouin zone carries Berry curvature.
