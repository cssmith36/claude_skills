# arXiv digest — 2026-10-28    (2 core, 2 relevant)

## Core
### Chiral Superconductivity Adjacent to a Wigner Crystal in Rhombohedral Graphene
Ke Wang  ·  cond-mat.supr-con  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33808
Why: Hartree–Fock finds a triangular Wigner crystal in rhombohedral tetralayer graphene, and a parquet/Bethe–Salpeter treatment finds chiral f-wave pairing in the adjacent metal. It links Wigner crystallization to superconductivity in a graphene moiré-family system. No NQS.

### Second-Harmonic Bulk Response of Fractional Quantum Hall Fluids
J. Luke Pimlott, Pablo Reiser, T. Hans Hansson, Habib Rostami  ·  cond-mat.mes-hall  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33606
Why: Composite-boson Chern–Simons theory for the nonlinear Hall response of LLL FQH fluids with broken inversion and rotation symmetry. Analytic theory, not NQS. Included for recall on the FQH line.

## Also relevant
### Fermionic superfluid transition and pairing scale separation in the kagome flat band
Xiang Li, Yu Wang  ·  cond-mat.str-el  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34342
Why: DQMC of the attractive kagome Hubbard model at flat-band filling finds T_c ∝ |U| and a wide gap between pair formation and phase coherence. Flat-band pairing, no NQS.

### The fate of $s_{\pm}$ superconductivity in the bilayer nickelate La$_3$Ni$_2$O$_7$
Kaustubh Roy, Piers Coleman  ·  cond-mat.supr-con  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35814
Why: A mean-field fractionalization of a bilayer t-J model gives a Coulomb pseudopotential that suppresses s± pairing. Unconventional superconductivity theory, no ML.
