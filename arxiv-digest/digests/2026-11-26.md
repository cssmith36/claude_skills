## Core
### Parametric neural-network wavefunctions across a bosonic phase transition
Daniil S. Antonenko, Liang Fu, Leonid I. Glazman  ·  cond-mat.quant-gas  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33150
Why: New real-space NQS architecture. "Boseformer" is an attention-based bosonic wavefunction, trained with VMC and conditioned on a Hamiltonian parameter. One trained model covers the superfluid-to-crystal transition of 2D bosons with 1/r³ repulsion. It's a parametric "foundation model" approach that could carry over to fermionic Wigner-crystal NQS.

### Augmented Imaginary-Time Evolution of Neural Quantum States with Implicit Time Steps
Maksymilian Kliczkowski, Thomas D. K\"uhne, …, Carlos L. Benavides-Riveros, Werner Dobrautz  ·  quant-ph  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32236
Why: NQS optimization method. An implicit-Euler, Galerkin-projected stochastic reconfiguration allows large, stable steps. On top of it, an implicit augmented imaginary-time evolution has asymptotically quadratic energy convergence and is reported to clearly beat standard SR.

### Superconductivity with valley-imbalanced Fermi surfaces in thick rhombohedral graphene
Le Liu, Jian Zhao, …, Yang Zhang, Weibo Gao  ·  cond-mat.supr-con  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31861
Why: Graphene superconductivity with a moiré angle. In hBN-aligned 17-layer rhombohedral graphene, an in-plane field enhances superconductivity out of a valley-imbalanced, spin-polarized parent metal. Superconductivity is suppressed when carriers move to the strongly moiré-reconstructed surface. The authors suggest equal-spin intervalley pairing.

### Topological Anderson Superconductor in Landau Levels
Uddalok Nag, Koji Kudo, J. K. Jain  ·  cond-mat.supr-con  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32988
Why: Superconductivity meets Landau levels. Self-consistent BdG for a Rashba superconductor in a magnetic field gives mostly even BdG Chern numbers. On-site disorder that mixes Landau levels lets topological superconductivity percolate in the transition regions.

### Topological Narrow Bands in Twisted Rhombohedral Multilayer Graphene
Alejandro Jimeno-Pozo, Danna Liu, Francisco Guinea, Pierre A. Pantale\'on  ·  cond-mat.mes-hall  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32229
Why: Moiré flat bands. In twisted monolayer/N-layer rhombohedral graphene, the central narrow bands come from a Dirac cone hybridizing with a rhombohedral surface state. Flipping the displacement field switches the lower band's Chern number between C=N and C=−1.

### Moir\'e-Induced Interlayer Electron-Phonon Coupling in Graphene/hBN Superlattices
Dasol Kim, Zhengguang Lu, …, Feng Wang, Long Ju  ·  cond-mat.mes-hall  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31896
Why: Experiment on moiré flat bands. Infrared photocurrent shows the hBN out-of-plane (ZO) phonon forming a Fano resonance with moiré flat-band excitations, direct evidence of enhanced interlayer electron-phonon coupling.

### High-density correlation energy of the electron gas
Miguel A. L. Marques, Paulo R. Pires, Stefano Pittalis  ·  cond-mat.str-el  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31426
Why: Electron-gas correlation energy used as DFT functional input. The r_s coefficient D of the 3D electron gas is recomputed as −0.00779 Ha, versus the older estimate of −0.010 Ha. Higher-order coefficients are computed too.
