## Core
### Autoregressive Projective Quantum Monte Carlo: From a Hermitian to a Non-Hermitian Perspective
Lavoisier Wah, Remmy Zen, Flore K. Kunst  ·  quant-ph  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09496
Why: Introduces an RNN-guided autoregressive sampling scheme for projective quantum Monte Carlo, an NQS training/sampling methods paper — reports consistently lower energies and higher fidelity than standard variational RNN ansätze on both Hermitian and non-Hermitian Hamiltonians.

### Relaxation-driven flat bands and topology in moiré transition metal dichalcogenide heterobilayers
Mitchell Luskin, Max Geier, Liang Fu, Ziyan Zhu  ·  cond-mat.mes-hall  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08917
Why: Shows lattice-relaxation pseudomagnetic fields (not the moiré potential) drive Chern bands in WSe2/WS2 heterobilayers, and confirms the gaps survive many-body interactions via neural-network variational Monte Carlo — direct overlap of moiré flat-band correlated physics and NQS/VMC methodology.

### Gradient-based optimization of non-Abelian fractional quantum states in patterned superlattices
Yifei Guan, Lichen Yu, …, Xin Lu, Jianpeng Liu  ·  cond-mat.mes-hall  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09245
Why: Gradient-descent inverse design of patterned dielectric superlattices on bilayer graphene to engineer flat Chern bands with first-excited-Landau-level-like quantum geometry, yielding non-Abelian fractional Chern insulator states at 1/2 filling — squarely in the moiré/quantum-Hall-family space.

### Tunable Multiband Geometry and Fractional Phases in Higher Vortexable Systems
Xiaohan Wan, Siddhartha Sarkar, …, Di Xiao, Kai Sun  ·  cond-mat.str-el  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09911
Why: Exact-diagonalization study of higher-vortexable moiré flat bands finds a cascade of Abelian and non-Abelian phases (fractional exciton insulators, Moore-Read, Read-Rezayi) at zero field, tunable purely via wavefunction quantum geometry — moiré flat-band physics directly adjacent to the FQH family.

### Nonequilibrium dynamics of doped Chern ferromagnets: a case study for false vacuum decay
Kilian Kuhlbrodt, Olivier Huber, …, Siddharth A. Parameswaran, Atac Imamoglu  ·  cond-mat.str-el  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08937
Why: Optically prepares and tracks true-vacuum bubbles inside metastable itinerant/Chern ferromagnet states in a moiré device, with strongly filling-dependent dynamics at integer and fractional Chern insulator fillings — moiré correlated-electron physics tied to the Chern insulator/quantum Hall family.

### Cell Natural Orbitals in Interacting Topological Bands
Nishchhal Verma, Harshitra Mahalingam, Daniel Muñoz-Segovia, Raquel Queiroz  ·  cond-mat.str-el  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09932
Why: Develops a geometry-based real-space orbital decomposition for projected interactions in topological flat bands, applied to magic-angle twisted bilayer graphene — moiré flat-band correlated-electron methodology.

### Spin Polarization of a Two-Dimensional Electron Liquid
G. A. Nikolaev, M. E. Sergeev, I. V. Kukushkin, A. V. Shchepetilnikov  ·  cond-mat.str-el  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09533
Why: Experimentally establishes a parameter-free relation between magnetotransport and spin polarization in a strongly correlated 2D electron system, mapping its magnetic state across density and field — direct 2DEG correlated-electron physics.
