### Sum rules and density-wave modes in spin-singlet fractional quantum Hall fluids
Ritajit Kundu, Rakesh K. Dora, Dung Xuan Nguyen, Ajit C. Balram  ·  cond-mat.str-el  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11133

### Composite-Boson Ansatz for Fractional Quantum Hall Manifolds of Lattice Bosons at Generic Fillings $ν<1/2$
Umut Can Turhan, A. Levent Subaşı, Rifat Onur Umucalılar  ·  cond-mat.quant-gas  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10747
Why: Builds a composite-boson variational trial-wavefunction ansatz for Hofstadter-Bose-Hubbard FQH manifolds and uses variational Monte Carlo to extend it to larger systems — quantum-Hall-family methods with a VMC angle.

### Some features of high-temperature superconductivity on flat bands
