## Core
### Analytical Interacting Tight-binding Model for Fractional Chern Insulators
Lexu Zhao, Jayden S. Chang, Awwab A. Azam, Jiabin Yu  ·  cond-mat.str-el  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23409
Why: A local 2-band lattice model whose exact ground states at 1/3 filling are FCI analogues of Laughlin states, with zero-energy quasihole states (like Laughlin quasiholes under a V_1 pseudopotential), and no band projection needed. Quantum Hall family; could be a clean exact benchmark for neural ansätze of FQH/FCI states.

## Also relevant
### Exact Parent Hamiltonians for Partially Magnetized Chiral Spin Liquids
Xin Shen, Guangyue Ji, Jie Wang  ·  cond-mat.str-el  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24935
Why: Builds exact Hubbard-type parent Hamiltonians for bosonic lattice FQH states (Abelian and non-Abelian parton) and maps them to chiral spin liquids, with realizations in Hofstadter-Hubbard models. Adjacent to the quantum Hall line.

_No NQS-methods, moiré-superconductivity, or Aharonov–Casher papers today._
