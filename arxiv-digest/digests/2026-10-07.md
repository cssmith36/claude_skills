## Core
### From Ground State to Electronic and Vibronic Spectra via Memory Kernel Coupling Theory and Neural Quantum States
Xinchen He, Wenjie Dou  ·  physics.chem-ph  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04315

### Disorder-driven loss of crystalline coherence in two-dimensional electron solids: Quantum Hartree-Fock and classical molecular dynamics
Sankar Das Sarma, Haining Pan  ·  cond-mat.dis-nn  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06371
Why: Wigner-solid physics under quenched disorder — Hartree-Fock and MD show disorder shrinks the crystalline coherence length (Imry-Ma/Larkin picture) and stabilizes an amorphous solid.

### Escaping the Composite Fermi Sea: An Incompressible State at Half Filling in Graphene's Lowest Landau Level
Domagoj Perkovic, Ziwei Wang, Steven Herbert Simon  ·  cond-mat.mes-hall  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03921
Why: Quantum Hall / composite-fermion problem (ED + iDMRG, RPA Landau-level mixing) finding a strongly paired composite-fermion phase as candidate for the even-denominator states in graphene; possible overlap with your QH line.

### Beyond the Flat-Band Paradigm: Open Orbits as a Route to Correlated States in Dispersive Moiré Minibands
Zihao Wang, Pablo M. Perez-Piskunow, …, Kostya S. Novoselov, Nicolas Leconte  ·  cond-mat.mes-hall  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06110
Why: Moiré graphene/hBN in weak magnetic fields showing transport signatures of incipient fractional Chern insulator behavior in dispersive (non-flat) bands; experimental.
