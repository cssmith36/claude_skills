## Core
### Anyonic Neural Network Wavefunctions
Andres Perez Fadon, Pok Man Tam, Leyna Shackleton  ·  cond-mat.str-el  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23815
Why: New continuum NQS ansatz ("AnyNet") that exactly enforces anyonic short-distance behavior, reaching N=16–20 anyons vs. N≤4 for ED. NQS-methods paper in the quantum-Hall/anyon neighborhood.

### Attraction from repulsion in an interacting Chern band: chiral superconductivity, generalized Hall crystals, and competing fractional Chern insulators
Fabian Pichler, Clemens Kuhlenkamp, Ashvin Vishwanath, Michael Knap  ·  cond-mat.str-el  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22498
Why: Tensor-network study of FCIs transitioning to chiral p-wave superconductivity and a generalized Hall crystal in a Chern band, explaining fluctuation-induced attraction (RPA + Hartree-Fock). Directly on the FCI/SC/crystal proximity seen in moiré experiments.

### Fractional Chern insulators and composite Fermi liquids vs. charge order in twisted MoTe$_2$
Enrique Aguilar-M\'endez, Glenn Wagner  ·  cond-mat.str-el  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22479
Why: Comprehensive ED of FCIs, composite Fermi liquids and charge order in twisted MoTe$_2$, including band mixing needed to match experiment. Moiré/flat-band + quantum-Hall-family physics.

### Anyon bound states in MoTe$_2$ Fractional Chern Insulators
Tianhong Lu, Luiz H. Santos  ·  cond-mat.str-el  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22473
Why: ED of exciton–e/3 quasihole bound states in a twisted MoTe$_2$ FCI, with real-space charge/quantum-geometry structure of the quasihole. Moiré FCI physics; no ML.
