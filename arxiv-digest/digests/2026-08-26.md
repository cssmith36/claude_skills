### Importance-Reweighted Fock-Space Variational Monte Carlo
Zheng Che  ·  physics.chem-ph  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22925
Why: Redesigns the Monte Carlo evaluation of Fock-space VMC for ab initio electronic Hamiltonians — a Hamiltonian-guided proposal plus self-normalized importance reweighting to recover Born-distribution expectations for the energy, gradient, and stochastic-reconfiguration matrix — an NQS/VMC sampling-and-optimization methods paper (H2O, hydrogen lattices, Fe2S2/Fe4S4 active spaces).
