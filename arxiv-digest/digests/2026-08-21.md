### Emergent BEC mechanism in flat-band superconductors

Muhammad Faisal, Micheal Vogl  ·  cond-mat.mes-hall  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18945
Why: Proposes generating tunable flat bands and valley-Chern topological transitions in bilayer graphene via patterned light instead of a physical twist, explicitly targeting the same flat-band correlated/topological physics as moiré superlattices.
