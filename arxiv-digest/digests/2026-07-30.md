### Van Hove singularity-driven giant Nernst signal in twisted double bilayer graphene
Ujjal Roy, Monosij Roy, …, Subroto Mukerjee, Anindya Das  ·  cond-mat.mes-hall  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25359
