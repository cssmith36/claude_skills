### Continuum neural wavefunctions for anyons on a torus
Ahmed Khalifa, Shubhayu Chatterjee  ·  cond-mat.str-el  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36567
Why: AnyFormer is a continuum neural variational (VMC) ansatz with torus boundary constraints, scaled to N=16 semions beyond continuum ED. Close in spirit to NQS for FQH/anyonic physics — check overlap with your quantum Hall work.

### Agentic Swarms Discover Enhanchment of Fractional Chern Insulator Gaps Beyond Ideal Quantum Geometry
Yitong Zhang, Changwen Xu, …, Kai Sun, Venkatasubramanian Viswanathan  ·  cond-mat.str-el  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35934
Why: Multi-agent AI study finding fractional-topological-order gaps in flat bands grow by >10× when departing from ideal quantum geometry, with the real-space quantum metric as the controlling quantity. Flat-band/FCI physics; methods are AI-agent driven rather than NQS.
