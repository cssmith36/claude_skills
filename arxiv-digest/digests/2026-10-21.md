### Variational Block Correlated Coupled Cluster Monte Carlo
Hunter Brodie, Alexander Shilcusky, Adrian E. Feiguin  ·  cond-mat.str-el  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26646
Why: Variational coupled-cluster wavefunction optimized with Monte Carlo, explicitly motivated by quantum neural states; new Young-tableaux representation cuts optimization cost and a fictitious-temperature importance sampling improves convergence (tested on 1D/2D Heisenberg).

### Vortexable Chern Bands Sit Exactly on the Topological Floor of Flat-Band Superfluid Stiffness
Zhi-Wei Wang, Samuel L. Braunstein  ·  cond-mat.supr-con  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24877
Why: Flat-band superconductivity in vortexable Chern bands (the ideal-FCI/quantum-Hall-like setting); gives the exact T=0 superfluid stiffness lower bound set by Chern number and shows enhancement costs vortexability.

### Theory of High-Temperature Fractional Chern Phases in Monolayer van der Waals Kagome Materials A$_2$T$_3$X$_4$
Soomin Oh, Jiyong Han, …, Mingu Kang, Gil Young Cho  ·  cond-mat.str-el  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24633
Why: Exact diagonalization on DFT-derived topological flat-band models finds fractional Chern insulators, anomalous composite Fermi liquids and charge-ordered phases (gap ~26 meV in Cs$_2$Pd$_3$S$_4$) — overlaps the FQH/composite-fermion/flat-band line.
