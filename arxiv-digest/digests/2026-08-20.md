# arXiv digest — 2026-08-20    (3 core, 2 relevant)

## Core
### Quantum Geometric Tensor Preconditioning for Stable Training of Recurrent Neural Quantum States
Adil Attar, Amine M. Aboussalah, Mohamed Hibat-Allah  ·  cond-mat.dis-nn  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18065
Why: NQS methods paper — stabilizes the minSR curvature-based optimizer for RNN-based neural quantum states via regularization, outperforming Adam on the 1D transverse-field Ising and cluster-state models and giving competitive results on 2D Heisenberg/J1-J2. A training/optimization advance, CORE per the methods-are-CORE rule regardless of physics target.

### Electronic Reconstruction at the Quasicrystal-Moiré Crossover in Twisted Bilayer Graphene
Kuo-En Chang, Aitor Garcia-Ruiz, …, Ming-Hao Liu, Tse-Ming Chen  ·  cond-mat.mes-hall  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18052
Why: STEM and magnetotransport study of large-angle twisted bilayer graphene revealing electronic reconstruction and a highly unconventional, temperature-dependent Landau-level spectrum near the quasicrystal-to-moiré crossover — squarely in the moiré/flat-band correlated-electron family.

### Many-Anyon Braiding in Non-Abelian Fractional Quantum Hall Effect with Hybrid Monte Carlo Simulation
Ting-Tung Wang, Ha Quang Trung, …, Bo Yang, Zi Yang Meng  ·  cond-mat.str-el  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17424
Why: Computes many-anyon non-Abelian braiding matrices for Moore-Read quasiholes via hybrid Monte Carlo and explicitly ties the result to fractional quantum anomalous Hall states in moiré materials — core quantum-Hall-family physics.

## Also relevant
### Entropy mapping under uniaxial pressure utilizing the elastocaloric effect
Zhenhai Hu, You-Sheng Li, …, Michael Nicklas, Andreas W. Rost  ·  cond-mat.supr-con  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17777
Why: Reconstructs an absolute entropy map across the strain phase diagram of the unconventional superconductor Sr2RuO4, showing entropy quenching is strongest near the critical strain where the gap is maximized at the Van Hove singularity — unconventional superconductivity physics, no NQS/ML angle.

### Bridging ambient- and high-pressure superconductivity in La2LnNi2O7 films
Motoki Osada, Chieko Terakura, …, Yoshinori Tokura, Atsushi Tsukazaki  ·  cond-mat.supr-con  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17745
Why: Tracks Tc across ambient- and high-pressure regimes in strained bilayer nickelate films, correlating enhancement from 41-42 K to 67-73 K under pressure with normal-state T^2-to-T-linear transport — unconventional high-Tc superconductivity physics.
