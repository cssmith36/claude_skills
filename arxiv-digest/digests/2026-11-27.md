## Core
### Pairing from anti-Hund's coupling under infinite Hubbard repulsion
Donggyu Kim, Ya-Hui Zhang  ·  cond-mat.str-el  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33286
Why: Pairing in a Hubbard-type model, with possible application to magic-angle TBG. In a U=∞ two-orbital triangular-lattice model, DMRG finds robust bond pairing driven by a phonon-mediated anti-Hund's coupling, with a non-BCS strong-coupling theory for large J_A.

### Hidden f-wave TIVC and Quantum Phase Transitions in Metallic States of Twisted Bilayer Graphene
Arindam Pramanik, Unmesh Ghorai, Rajdeep Sensarma  ·  cond-mat.str-el  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34225
Why: Magic-angle TBG phase diagram for ν=−2 to −3. The KIVC phase hides f-wave TIVC order that grows in the metallic regime, and density tuning drives transitions IVC → valley-polarized → QAH.

### Fractional Chern Insulator in a Finite-Range One-Band Model
Tong Liu, Hoi Chun Po  ·  cond-mat.str-el  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34255
Why: FQH family. An FCI with no single-particle quantum geometry, from a plaquette-local interaction-only square-lattice model at 1/3 filling (ED + DMRG give σ_xy = −2e²/3h). The topology is traced to the repulsive two-particle sector.

### Emergent Chern metal in strongly correlated moir\'e bilayers
Kaushal Kumar Kesharpu, Evgenii A. Kochetov  ·  cond-mat.str-el  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34780
Why: Correlated moiré TMDs (tMoTe₂/WSe₂). Holons moving through a helical spinon texture form a C=±1 Chern metal, giving a non-quantized anomalous Hall response.

### Spin Loop Currents and Bulk-Edge Structure in Correlated Topological States of WSe$_2$/MoTe$_2$ Bilayers
Po-Jui Chen, Kevin P. Lucht, …, J. H. Pixley, Daniele Guerci  ·  cond-mat.str-el  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34880
Why: Correlated TMD heterobilayer. In parton mean field, non-local interactions drive a topological Kondo semimetal → insulator transition at ν=2 through an emergent spin-loop current.

### A microscopic continuum model for moir\'e transition metal dichalcogenides
Julian Ingham, Harshitra Mahalingam, Raquel Queiroz  ·  cond-mat.str-el  ·  https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34937
Why: An angle-independent continuum model for tTMDs, which host FCIs and superconductivity. It is a perturbation of a chiral magic-angle model, which explains the near-ideal quantum geometry, and it predicts an anomalous Landau-level slope. Likely a useful Hamiltonian for real-space NQS on tTMDs.
